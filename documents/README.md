# Documents

The primary technical sources for the SAGITTARIUS project: its **full academic design report** and the **published conference paper** describing the simulation-coupled concurrent-engineering methodology.

## Published paper

**A Simulation-Coupled Concurrent Engineering Approach to Next-Generation Low-Cost Counter-UAV Missiles**  
Vincenzo Jr. Di Rosa, Francesco Gasperini, Matteo Giannitti, Michele Guida, Simone Gubbioni, Emanuele Cofani  
*Materials Research Proceedings*, **69**, 499–503 (2026). CEAS–AIDAA Conference 2025.

**[Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.21741/9781644904251-90)** · **[Read local PDF](SAGITTARIUS_paper_AIDAA.pdf)**

The paper presents the underlying methodology: linking mission assumptions and subsystem studies through a simulation-based, concurrent design process, then comparing reference and off-design cases.
